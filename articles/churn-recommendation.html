Business challenge

Business intelligence and CRM teams needed a predictive mechanism to identify player churn risk and deliver dynamic, contextual in-app content to improve engagement.

Architecture strategy

The project designed a unified real-time analytics data loop integrating high-throughput streaming pipelines with predictive scoring engines. End-to-end pipelines ingest user event patterns, compute behavioral vectors and deliver feature datasets to analytical scoring engines.

Scoring and recommendations

Predictive classification models produce real-time churn propensity signals. A hybrid collaborative recommendation algorithm generates player behavioral scores to support contextual recommendations.

Data architecture considerations

The architecture links event ingestion, behavioral feature generation, analytical scoring and downstream consumption. Data quality, feature consistency and timely delivery are important across each stage of this loop.

Business outcomes

The résumé reports a 15% improvement in marketing retention campaign performance, a 12% increase in player session durations and a 10% improvement in Day-7 retention metrics.

Architecture decisions & trade-offs

These records explain the reasoning behind the pattern and the trade-offs an architect should make explicit. They distinguish documented project choices from general design implications.

Behavioral feature pipeline vs. raw-event model input

A feature layer converts event histories into reusable analytical signals and separates event schemas from model inputs. The trade-off is feature freshness, consistency and lineage to source events.

Portfolio evidence: behavioral vectors and feature datasets are described.

Churn classification + recommendations vs. one combined score

Separate capabilities allow churn risk and recommendation relevance to be evaluated for different purposes. The trade-off is coordinating outputs and avoiding conflicting or repetitive activation.

Portfolio evidence: predictive churn classification and collaborative recommendations are described.

Real-time signals vs. periodic scoring

Fresh signals can support timely contextual actions. The trade-off is compute cost, serving complexity and the need to define acceptable freshness by use case.

Portfolio evidence: the project is described as a real-time analytics data loop.

Campaign lift measurement vs. model metrics alone

Business outcomes such as retention and session duration connect the data product to business value. The trade-off is attribution: campaign design and experimentation affect measured lift.

Portfolio evidence: résumé reports 15% retention campaign performance improvement, 12% session duration increase and 10% Day-7 retention improvement.

Architect’s takeaway

AI-enabled data architecture connects reliable behavioral data pipelines to scoring and activation systems. Its value should be measured through both technical performance and business outcomes.
